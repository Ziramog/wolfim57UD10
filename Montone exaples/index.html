01

/design advisory & strategy

I can help you make sharper product decisions, untangle design debt, and build a clear direction - so your team starts shipping with confidence. Having built products like Storefront UI and CommerceBooster UI from the ground up, I know what it takes not just to deliver, but to maintain and evolve a product over time.

02

/creative direction & ux/ui

From early concept to polished interface. I shape the product experience — information architecture, interaction patterns, visual language — and art-direct execution so design and brand stay coherent at every touchpoint.

03

/designing for the ai era

I use AI to compress timelines, not corners — vibe-coding interactive prototypes, AI-powered eCommerce UX, AI-assisted research, and embedding AI into design workflows. Iteration that used to take weeks now happens in days.

04

/design systems & frameworks

Scalable component libraries and design languages built to last. Whether you're starting from scratch or inheriting chaos, I build systems that let your team move faster without breaking consistency.

05

/ecommerce experience design

15+ years designing for commerce — from headless storefronts to checkout optimisation. I know what converts, what builds trust, and how to design for the full purchase journey across platforms and frameworks.

A comprehensive, open-source UI component library powering hundreds of eCommerce projects. Co-designed the first version of the design system — including component library, visual language, and documentation standards — now used by thousands of developers globally.

A complex workflow management tool for enterprise teams — bringing clarity to dense information hierarchies through systematic design thinking and component-driven UI.

A full eCommerce redesign for a Polish fashion retail brand — overhauling mobile UX, product listings, and checkout flow, delivering 70% YoY revenue growth and a 24% conversion uplift within 8 weeks.

/click & collect accelerator

A retail UX accelerator streamlining the buy-online, pick-up-in-store experience — from browsing and checkout to in-store handoff flows designed for speed and clarity.
